Terms and Policy - Dhaka Spice House

1. Order Confirmation: An order is confirmed only after the customer verbally or textually confirms the final cart summary.
2. Cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 5 minutes of placing the order. After that, a 20% cancellation fee applies.
3. Refunds: Refunds for incorrect or missing items are processed within 3-5 business days to the original payment method.
4. Spice Level: Customers may request mild, medium, or hot spice levels for applicable dishes at no extra charge.
5. Allergies: Please inform us of any food allergies before ordering; we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free kitchen.
6. Delivery Delays: In case of delays beyond 90 minutes, customers are entitled to a 10% discount on that order.
7. Minimum Order: Minimum order value for delivery is 200 BDT.
